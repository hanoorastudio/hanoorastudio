07 — FAQ

The questions
that come up most.

Something not covered here? Ask us directly and we'll get back within a day.

How quickly does work start after signing?+

Onboarding takes two to three working days. Your first content calendar is with you inside the first week, and the first assets are delivered within ten working days.

Is design really included in every tier?+

Yes. Feed layouts, banners, thumbnails and ad creative ship alongside the film and photography. Brand identity and packaging sit within Signature and Bespoke, where the scope calls for it.

Where is the studio based?+

We are based in Indonesia and work worldwide. Because the pipeline is remote, location has never been the constraint — we have delivered for brands across seven countries and schedule reviews around your time zone.

Do I need to ship you the product?+

Not on Essential or Signature — those run entirely from your photographs, packaging artwork, dielines or 3D files. The sharper the reference, the closer the result. If a brief genuinely needs the real object in front of a real camera, that lives in Bespoke.

What happens if I need to pause or stop?+

Retainers run month to month with 30 days' notice. You can pause between campaigns and pick up again when you're ready — no long lock-in, no penalty.

Do we own the work you make?+

Fully. Final masters, source files and full commercial usage rights are handed over at the end of each month. The work is yours outright.
